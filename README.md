# PDF Optimizer

Make big PDFs smaller, especially scans and photos of documents, right on
your phone. Nothing is uploaded: your files never leave your device.

**Open it here: https://austinkernel.github.io/pdf-optimizer/**

## Install it

It's a web app, so there's no App Store download. Add it to your Home Screen
and it works like a normal app, even offline.

**iPhone / iPad**
1. Open the link above in **Safari**.
2. Tap **Share** (the square with an arrow), then **Add to Home Screen**.

**Android**
1. Open the link above in **Chrome** (or Edge / Samsung Internet).
2. Tap **Install app** on the page, or open the menu **⋮** and choose
   **Install app** / **Add to Home screen**.

Open it once while you're online so it can save itself for offline use.

## Use it

1. Tap **Choose PDF** and pick a file.
2. Pick how much to shrink it:
   - **Lossless**: tidies the file; images untouched.
   - **High Quality**: smaller, looks identical.
   - **Balanced** (recommended): much smaller, no visible difference.
   - **Compact**: smallest; text stays readable.
3. Tap **Compress**. Keep the app open until it finishes.
4. Check the result, then tap **Save to Files / Share**.

Your original file is never changed. The compressed copy is a new file.

## Privacy

- Everything happens on your device. No uploads, accounts, ads or tracking.
- The app shows how many network requests it made while processing: zero.

## Updates

The app updates itself. When a new version is ready, **Update ready** appears
next to **App info & updates** at the bottom of the main screen. Open it and
tap **Install update now**.

## Good to know

- Searchable text, links and bookmarks in your PDF are kept.
- Black-and-white scans and password-protected PDFs aren't shrunk yet.
- Very large files can hit the phone's memory limit for web apps.
